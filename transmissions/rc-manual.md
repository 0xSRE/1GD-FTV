# Toyota RC-Series 6-Speed Manual (RC60 / RC61)

The 6-speed manual used with the 1GD-FTV in the Hilux and Fortuner (RC61/RC61F), and in the H300 Hiace (RC60J).

## Variants

| Code | Drive | 1GD-FTV applications | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| RC61 | 2WD | Hilux GUN136, Fortuner GUN165 (2.8 2WD) | [Spec sheet] |
| RC61F | 4WD | Hilux GUN126, Fortuner GUN156 (2.8 4WD) | [Spec sheet] |
| RC60J | 2WD, remote cable shift | Hiace H300 (GDH300/301/320/321/322/328), 2019– | [Spec sheet] |

Related units (not 1GD): RC60/RC60F behind the 2.4 L 2GD-FTV, and RC62F in the 3.5 L V6 Tacoma. The 1GD, 2GD, 1KD and 2KD share a bellhousing pattern.

Example part number: RC61F 4WD assembly **33030-0KE40** (Hilux) [Unverified].

## Gear ratios

| Gear | RC60 / RC60J / RC60F | RC61 / RC61F |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 4.784 | 4.784 |
| 2nd | 2.423 | 2.423 |
| 3rd | 1.443 | 1.443 |
| 4th | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| 5th | 0.777 | 0.826 |
| 6th | 0.643 | 0.643 |
| Reverse | 4.066 | 4.066 |

Source: [Spec sheet] (Wikipedia). RC60 and RC61 differ only in 5th gear.

## Notes

- **Torque rating.** The original manual Hilux was rated at 420 Nm, compared with 450 Nm for the automatic, to protect the clutch and gearbox. After the 2020 update the manual is rated up to 500 Nm in most markets [Unverified]. Check your market's rating.
- **Low 1st gear.** With a 4.784 first gear and low range, the manual crawls more slowly than the automatic. Many owners prefer it off-road.
- **Clutch and dual-mass flywheel (DMF).** The 1GD manual uses a DMF. Rattling at idle or judder when taking off usually means the DMF is worn. Many owners replace the clutch and DMF together, or fit a solid-flywheel conversion kit.
- **Oil.** Toyota specifies a manual transmission gear oil (API GL-4/GL-5, 75W-90 in most markets). Confirm the grade and capacity in the owner's manual.
