# 8-Speed Automatic (Land Cruiser Prado 250)

The Prado 250 (2024–) pairs the 1GD-FTV (about 150 kW / 500 Nm) with an **8-speed automatic**. Toyota calls it "Direct Shift-8AT" in some markets [Spec sheet, single source].

## What's known

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Gears | 8 forward | [Spec sheet] |
| Engine pairing | 1GD-FTV 2.8 diesel, also offered with a 48 V mild-hybrid system in some markets | [Spec sheet] |
| Why it changed | Closer ratios and more gears fix the sluggish, "busy" feel of the old AC60 6-speed | [Community] |
| Manual option | None | [Spec sheet] |
